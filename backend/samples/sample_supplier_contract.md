Supplier agreement: Lanka Print and Finebase

This is a sample contract for tests and demos. It is not a real agreement.

This agreement is between Finebase (Pvt) Ltd and Lanka Print (Pvt) Ltd. It starts on 1 October 2026 and ends on 30 September 2027.

Lanka Print prints and delivers the Finebase user manuals. The monthly fee is LKR 40,000, paid within 30 days of each invoice.

Either party may end this agreement with 60 days written notice. Late delivery earns Finebase a credit of 5 percent of that month's fee.
